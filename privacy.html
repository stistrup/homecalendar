Privacy policy

Stistrup HomeCalendar is a personal desktop application used only by its owner.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google Calendar, through the Google Calendar API, to display, create, edit and delete events.
  • Calendar data and the access token stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any other server, sold or shared with third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
